Regional reach and field deployment

Geo-Sector Command Centers · GSCCs

Regional hubs implementing protocols, collecting local data and coordinating DCO, DSA and DSH initiatives. They report to the Central Nexus.

Field Operatives

Three classifications: Tier 1 Observation & Analysis (Omega-3); Tier 2 Intervention & Implementation (Omega-4); Tier 3 Tactical & Suppression (Omega-5).

Covert Insertion and Extraction Teams · CIETs

Specialised teams described for infiltration, extraction and threat-neutralisation missions; compartmentalised within the manual's institution.

Internal oversight

Internal Compliance Division 7 · ICD-7

The manual describes ICD-7 as an autonomous oversight body, to a degree even from the Directorate. It monitors personnel through NLI fidelity checks, biometric tracking, communications analysis and peer reporting, and handles re-education, service re-allocation and internal threat assessment.

The source describes ICD-7 as operating with “absolute autonomy, even from the Directorate to a degree.” The map therefore presents it as an oversight body, not simply as another division beneath the executive tiers.

How to read this map

The manual states that the five divisions are interconnected. It describes the Directorate, Inner Council and executive tiers as a command hierarchy; regional centres and field operatives as the global implementation network; and ICD-7 as an autonomous internal oversight body. The chart intentionally avoids inventing a strict one-to-one reporting chain between every division and every field unit.

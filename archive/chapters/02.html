Chapter 2 describes how the DHIE is organised to carry out the mission established in Chapter 1. Its structure is presented as a centralised command system: the Directorate sets direction; the Inner Council translates that direction into policy; executive tiers coordinate implementation; five divisions specialise in different domains; regional bureaus and field operatives extend the organisation's reach; and ICD-7 monitors the organisation's own personnel.

2.1 · The Directorate: Command and Control

The Directorate sits at the apex of the structure. The source assigns it responsibility for conceiving, approving and overseeing major evolutionary directives.

2.1.1Director Cican George Alin: The Chief Architect

The manual places Director Cican George Alin at the absolute top of the hierarchy and identifies him as the “Chief Architect” of directed evolution. It assigns him final approval over critical evolutionary directives, major resource reallocations and strategic threat assessments. His base is the Central Nexus, where the text says his immediate staff hold Omega-7 clearance and absolute Neural-Linguistic Imprint (NLI) fidelity. These descriptions establish the Director as both the source of doctrine and the final decision-maker in the fictional system.

2.1.2The Inner Council (The Octad): Strategic Policy Makers

The Inner Council, called “The Octad,” consists of eight individuals selected by the Director. The source assigns the council four principal functions: translating the Director's vision into operational protocols; adjudicating disputes over resources between divisions; conducting high-level threat assessments and countermeasure planning; and overseeing long-term Evolutionary Trajectory models generated by Oracle. Its meetings are described as taking place in secure, quantum-encrypted chambers inside The Core, with records stored on ephemeral data streams. Each member exercises Directorate-level authority within a designated sphere.

2.1.3Central Nexus Command Hierarchy: Layers of Authority

Beneath the Octad, the manual sets out three executive tiers. Alpha comprises senior directors and chief strategists with Omega-6 clearance, responsible for divisional objectives and large projects. Beta comprises principal analysts and lead researchers with Omega-5 clearance, developing protocols and managing research teams. Gamma comprises operational leads and section managers with Omega-4 clearance, overseeing regional implementation and field deployments. Promotion is described as dependent not on seniority but on adherence to DHIE principles, analytical ability and NLI fidelity; failure can lead to reassessment, reassignment or cognitive dissolution.

2.2 · Core Divisions: Pillars of DHIE Operations

Five specialised divisions form the operational centre of the Department. Their mandates are distinct, but the manual presents them as interconnected components of a single system.

DCO · Division of Cognitive OptimizationThe mind-focused division: cognitive pathways, intelligence, memory, analytical ability and decision-making.

2.2.1Division of Cognitive Optimization (DCO): The Mind Sculptors

DCO is responsible for managing and enhancing mental faculties. Its listed mandates include neuro-augmentation, synaptic pruning and rewiring, accelerated learning, dream-state programming and cognitive-bias correction. Its stated focus is neurological intervention. The chapter names Project Cerebrum, described as a global IQ uplift, and the Omniscience Initiative, associated with memory integration.

DSA · Division of Somatic AdaptationThe body-focused division: genetic management, physiological augmentation, lifespan extension and reproductive oversight.

2.2.2Division of Somatic Adaptation (DSA): The Body Forgers

DSA is assigned the biological and physiological domain. The manual lists Genetic Trajectory Management (GTMs), Physiological Augmentation (PAAs), Lifespan Extension and Immortality Protocols (LEIPs), and Reproductive Oversight and Population Control (ROPCs) among its mandates. Its stated focus includes bio-engineering, genetic manipulation, organ regeneration and environmental adaptation. Named projects are the Omni-Immunity Project and Project Titan, associated respectively with disease eradication and musculoskeletal enhancement.

DSH · Division of Social HarmonizationThe society-focused division: behavioural conditioning, public perception, information management and social coordination.

2.2.3Division of Social Harmonization (DSH): The Architects of Society

DSH is tasked with shaping societal structures and behaviour at population scale. Its mandates include Social Harmonization and Compliance Protocols (SHCPs), Education and Propaganda Management (EPMs), Resource Allocation and Equity Frameworks (RAEFs), and Anomaly Detection and Containment (ADCs). The source identifies behavioural conditioning, information control, social-credit systems and conflict management as its focus. It names the Global Consensus Initiative and Project Serenity as examples.

DET · Division of Evolutionary TrajectoryThe forecasting division: future modelling, evolutionary risk assessment and long-range species planning.

2.2.4Division of Evolutionary Trajectory (DET): The Future Seers

DET is described as the Department's foresight engine. It uses Oracle AI to model possible timelines, identify evolutionary bottlenecks and chart a long-term path towards Homo Optima. Its mandates include predictive modelling, risk assessment for evolutionary deviations, long-term species planning, inter-species development projections and post-humanity scenarios. The chapter names the Genesis Simulation and Project Stellar Seed as examples of its work.

DHR · Division of Historical RevisionThe history-and-memory division: archives, public records, collective memory and approved historical narratives.

2.2.5Division of Historical Revision (DHR): The Custodians of Truth

DHR is presented as the division responsible for curating, modifying or fabricating historical narratives to align with the Department's objectives. Its mandates include Memory Calibration and Recall (MCRs), archival revision, public-record management and propaganda dissemination. The manual describes its central function as shaping collective memory and the approved understanding of history. Named projects are Project Chronos, a proposed global historical database rewrite, and the Baseline Memory Imprint, associated with standardised childhood memories.

2.3 · Regional Bureaus and Field Operatives: Global Reach

The chapter extends the organisational diagram beyond headquarters, describing a regional network and personnel embedded across society.

2.3.1Geo-Sector Command Centers

The planet is divided in the manual's fictional model into Geo-Sector Command Centers (GSCCs). These regional hubs implement Department protocols, collect local data, coordinate DCO, DSA and DSH initiatives, and oversee local anomaly detection. They have some autonomy to adapt operations to local conditions but report to the Central Nexus, with activity monitored and data sent to Minerva in real time.

2.3.2Field Operative Classification and Deployment

Field Operatives are divided into three tiers. Tier 1 handles observation and analysis, including covert placement in communities, surveillance and data collection, with Omega-3 clearance. Tier 2 handles intervention and implementation of protocols, including the source's examples of cognitive re-education, somatic therapies and social re-patterning, with Omega-4 clearance. Tier 3 handles tactical work and suppression, including anomaly containment and direct intervention in perceived threats, with Omega-5 clearance. The manual says operatives undergo psychological conditioning and NLI reinforcement.

2.3.3Covert Insertion and Extraction Teams

Covert Insertion and Extraction Teams (CIETs) are described as elite units used for missions requiring discretion. Their stated roles include infiltrating resistant groups, extracting high-value subjects for re-education or study, and neutralising threats to DHIE operations. The source depicts them as highly enhanced specialists whose existence and operations are compartmentalised, known only to senior leadership and relevant divisional leads.

2.4 · Internal Compliance Division (ICD-7): The Enforcers of Fidelity

The final section turns the Department's oversight inward. ICD-7 is described as an autonomous body charged with monitoring loyalty and preventing internal corruption or ideological deviation.

2.4.1Surveillance and Monitoring Protocols for Personnel

The manual lists four forms of personnel monitoring: involuntary NLI fidelity checks for ideological alignment; Biometric Signature Tracking (BST) for location and physiological or emotional states; interception and analysis of internal communications; and peer-to-peer reporting requirements. The collected data is said to feed a specialised Minerva subsystem for internal threat assessment.

2.4.2Re-Education and Service Re-Allocation for Violations

According to the source, minor violations can trigger re-education involving psychological conditioning and memory calibration. More serious breaches can lead to Service Re-Allocation: removal from sensitive duties, reassignment to menial work, or transfer to controlled environments for study and containment. This section makes clear that the organisation's compliance system applies to its own operatives as well as the populations it governs in the narrative.

2.4.3Threat Assessment and Suppression of Internal Dissent

ICD-7 is described as proactively searching for resistance movements or ideological cells within the Department. The manual lists suppression measures ranging from memory alteration and psychological re-patterning to “Cognitive Dissolution Procedures,” described in the source as irreversible eradication of consciousness. The section closes by presenting loyalty as something engineered and enforced, not simply expected.

Chapter 2 in one sentence: The manual depicts a vertically integrated institution in which central leadership sets policy, five divisions manage specialised domains, regional and field structures implement directives, and ICD-7 enforces internal fidelity.

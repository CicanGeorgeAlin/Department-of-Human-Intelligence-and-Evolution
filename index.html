Inside the manual

A blueprint for a deliberately shaped future.

Presented as an internal manual, the book develops an imagined system for directing human development. Its four-part structure moves from the foundations of oversight to the cognitive, somatic and environmental dimensions of its vision.

PART I · FOUNDATIONS

The imperative of evolutionary oversight

The origins, doctrine, organizational structure and data-acquisition methods of the imagined DHIE.

PART II · COGNITIVE REALM

Shaping the mind

Cognitive Optimization, Emotional Harmonization, Creativity and Innovation, and Memory Calibration.

PART III · SOMATIC REALM

Shaping the body

Genetic Trajectory Management, Reproductive Oversight, and Physiological Augmentation.

PART IV · ENVIRONMENTAL REALM

Shaping the world

Resource Allocation, Habitat Engineering, Surveillance and Social Credit, and the idea of Homo Optima.

A question at the heart of DHIE
